## Introduction

Everything in **TestCLIX** happens inside a **project**. Tests, results, schedules, integrations, team members, and billing are all scoped to the project you are currently working in.

A project brings together:

- **Workspace** - directories, tests, and their results
- **Team** - members, their roles, and pending invitations
- **Integrations** - [Slack](/integration/slack), [Telegram](/integration/telegram), [Jira](/integration/jira), [webhooks](/integration/webhooks), [email notifications](/integration/emails), and the project [API key](/integration/api)
- **Billing** - subscription, invoices, and purchases

You can be a member of multiple projects, and your role may be different in each one. See [Roles & Permissions](/project-management/permissions) for what each role can do.

This guide explains:

- How projects are created and switched
- How to rename a project
- How to leave a project

## Your First Project

When you sign up, TestCLIX automatically creates a personal workspace project for you, named after your email address (for example, `john workspace`). You are the **Owner** of this project and can rename it at any time.

If you were invited to an existing project instead, see [Accepting an Invitation](/project-management/users-and-invitations#accepting-an-invitation).

## Creating a Project

<Steps>

1. Click **Project** at the top of the left sidebar.

2. In the **Select project** dialog, click **Create project**.

3. Enter a **Project name** (up to 64 characters).

4. Click **Create**.

</Steps>

You become the new project's **Owner**. The project appears in your project list - switch to it from the **Select project** dialog. If it is your only project, it is selected automatically.

:::note
Each account can create a limited number of projects (5 by default). If you reach the limit, contact us at [clix@testclix.com](mailto:clix@testclix.com) to increase it.
:::

## Switching Projects

<Steps>

1. Click **Project** at the top of the left sidebar. The currently selected project is shown beneath the label.

2. In the **Select project** dialog, use **Search projects** or browse the **All projects** list. Each entry shows the project name and a shortened **Project ID** - search matches both, which helps when projects share similar names.

3. Click a project to switch to it. The application reloads and opens that project's **Dashboard**.

</Steps>

A few things to know:

- If you are a member of only **one** project, it is selected automatically when you sign in.
- If you are a member of **several** projects, TestCLIX shows the **Select project** page after sign-in so you can pick one.
- If you have **no projects at all** (for example, after leaving your last one), the **Select project** page shows an empty state with a **Create** button, so you can always create your first project from there.
- Your role and available actions may change after switching, because permissions are assigned **per project**.

## Renaming a Project

Renaming requires the **Owner** role.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** from the left sidebar menu and go to the **Project** tab.

2. In the **Project data** section, edit the **Project name** field (up to 64 characters).

3. Click **Update**.

</Steps>

<img src={projectSettings.src} alt="Settings → Project tab with Project data and Access & permissions sections" />

The **Project ID** shown above the name is read-only and identifies the project internally.

## Leaving a Project

Any member can leave a project, with one exception: the **only Owner** cannot leave.

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings** from the left sidebar menu and go to the **Project** tab.

2. In the **Access & permissions** section, click **Leave project**.

3. Confirm in the dialog.

</Steps>

After leaving, you are redirected to the **Select project** page. To rejoin the project later, you need a new invitation.

:::note
If you are the only Owner, the **Leave project** button is disabled and the message *"You are the only owner. You cannot leave the project."* is shown. Promote another member to **Owner** first.
:::

## Deleting a Project

Deleting a project is not available from the portal. If you need a project removed, contact us at [clix@testclix.com](mailto:clix@testclix.com).

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>I reached the project limit. How do I get more?</summary>

Contact us at [clix@testclix.com](mailto:clix@testclix.com) and we will increase your limit.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I rejoin a project after leaving it?</summary>

Yes, but only through a new invitation from that project's **Admin** or **Owner**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Do my role and permissions carry over between projects?</summary>

No.

Permissions are assigned **per project**, so your role may change after switching projects.

</details>
