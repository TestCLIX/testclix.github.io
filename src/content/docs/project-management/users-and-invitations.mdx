## Introduction

Team access in **TestCLIX** is managed on the **Users** page. Only **Admins** and **Owners** can invite users or change permissions.

Open **Users** from the left sidebar menu. The page contains two tabs:

| Tab             | Description                                 |
| ----------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Users**       | List of everyone who has joined the project |
| **Invitations** | Pending invitations not yet accepted        |

The **Project overview** panel on the left shows the project name, its ID, and your own permissions. In the **Users** tab, each member row shows the **Email** (your own row is marked with *(you)*), **Access** badges with the assigned role, and an **Actions** menu. Expand a row to see who invited that member and when they joined.

For a detailed breakdown of what each role can do, see [Roles & Permissions](/project-management/permissions).

## Inviting a User

<Steps>

1. Open **Users** from the left sidebar menu.

2. Click the **Invite user** button (user-plus icon) in the top-right corner.

3. In the **Invite user** dialog:
   - Enter the person's **email address**
   - Select a **role**: Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer, or Accountant
   - Optionally enable **Billing** for Admin, Editor, or Viewer
   - Click **View details** on a role card to see what that role includes

4. Click **Send invitation**.

   <img src={inviteUserDialog.src} alt="Invite user dialog with role selection" />

</Steps>

The invited person receives an email with a link to join the project.

### Invitation Rules

| Rule                    | Details                                                                              |
| ------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Expiry**              | Invitations expire after **7 days**                                                  |
| **One per email**       | An email address can have only one pending invitation per project                    |
| **Existing members**    | You cannot invite someone who is already a project member                            |
| **Email-bound**         | The invitation can only be accepted by the exact email address it was sent to        |
| **Owner role**          | Only an existing **Owner** can invite someone as **Owner**                           |

### Adding Billing Permission When Inviting

|Role selected                     | Billing option                                                                     |
|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|**Owner**                         | Always included                                                                    |
|**Accountant**                    | Always included                                                                    |
|**Admin**, **Editor**, **Viewer** | Optional - check **Billing** to also grant shop, invoices, and subscription access |

## Managing Pending Invitations

<Steps>

1. Open **Users** from the left sidebar menu and go to the **Invitations** tab.

    <img src={invitationsTab.src} alt="Invitations tab with pending invitations" />

2. Each pending invitation shows:
   - Email address
   - Assigned role (and **Billing** if applicable)
   - Who sent the invitation and when

3. Click **Cancel** to withdraw an invitation before it is accepted.

</Steps>

The **Invitations** tab shows a badge when invitations are pending.

:::note
There is no "resend" option. To send an invitation again (for example, after it expired), **Cancel** the pending one and invite the same email address again.
:::

## Accepting an Invitation

If you receive a project invitation:

<Steps>

1. Open the link from the invitation email.

2. If you are not signed in, click **Sign in to accept** and log in with the invited email. After signing in, you are returned to the invitation page.

3. Review the project name and who invited you.

4. Click **Accept invitation**. You are taken to the project's **Dashboard** automatically.

</Steps>

:::note
Expired or invalid invitations display an **Invitation unavailable** message. Ask an **Admin** or **Owner** of the project to invite you again.

If you are already a member of the project, accepting shows *"You are already a user of this project."* - you can simply open the project from the [project switcher](/project-management/overview#switching-projects).
:::

### If You Don't Have an Account Yet

You can be invited before creating a TestCLIX account. In that case:

<Steps>

1. From the sign-in page, use **Register now** to create an account with the **invited email address**.

2. Confirm your account.

3. Open the **invitation link from the email again** and click **Accept invitation**.

</Steps>

:::caution
The invitation is bound to the email address it was sent to. Signing in with a different email shows: *"This invitation was sent to a different email address."*
:::

## Editing Permissions

<Steps>

1. Open **Users** from the left sidebar menu and go to the **Users** tab.

2. Find the user in the table.

3. Click the **Actions** menu (three dots) on their row.

4. Select **Edit**.

5. In the **Edit role** dialog:
   - The email field is read-only
   - Select a new **role**
   - Adjust the **Billing** checkbox if applicable

6. Click **Update**.

   <img src={editRole.src} alt="Edit role dialog" />

</Steps>

Changes take effect immediately. If you edit **your own** role, the page reloads to apply the new permissions.

### Restrictions When Editing

| Situation                              | What happens                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| You are **Admin** (not Owner)          | You cannot assign the **Owner** role                                       |
| You are the **only Owner**             | You cannot edit or remove yourself as Owner                                |
| You are **Admin** editing an **Owner** | Edit is blocked                                                            |
| You want to remove **yourself**        | Use **Leave project** in **Settings** → **Project**, not **Delete member** |

### Transferring Ownership

There is no separate transfer flow - ownership is handed over by editing roles:

<Steps>

1. As an **Owner**, [edit the permissions](#editing-permissions) of the member who should take over and assign them the **Owner** role.

2. Once the project has more than one Owner, you can change your own role to something lower, or [leave the project](/project-management/overview#leaving-a-project).

</Steps>

## Removing a User

<Steps>

1. Open **Users** from the left sidebar menu and go to the **Users** tab.

2. Open the **Actions** menu (three dots) for that user.

3. Select **Delete member**.

4. Confirm in the dialog.

</Steps>

Removing another **Admin** or an **Owner** requires the **Owner** role.

:::note
You cannot remove yourself this way - use [Leave project](/project-management/overview#leaving-a-project) in **Settings** → **Project** instead. The last remaining **Owner** cannot be removed.
:::

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>The person I invited never received the email. What should I do?</summary>

Ask them to check their spam folder. If the email is missing, go to the **Invitations** tab, **Cancel** the pending invitation, and send a new one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change the role on a pending invitation?</summary>

No. **Cancel** the invitation and send a new one with the correct role.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How long is an invitation valid?</summary>

**7 days**. After that, the link shows **Invitation unavailable** and a new invitation must be sent.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why can't I assign the Owner role?</summary>

Only an existing **Owner** can promote another user to **Owner**.

</details>
