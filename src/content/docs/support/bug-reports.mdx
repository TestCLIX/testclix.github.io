---
title: Bug Reports
---

We do our best to provide the best possible product. However, sometimes we also make mistakes. Do not hesitate to contact us at `clix@testclix.com` if you find a bug. If you have any other suggestion, we are more than happy to discuss it as well.

## What to include

- A short summary of the issue.
- The page, feature, or workflow where the bug happened.
- The steps needed to reproduce it.
- What you expected to happen.
- What actually happened instead.
- Screenshots, screen recordings, or error messages if you have them.

## Helpful technical details

Including a few environment details helps us investigate faster:

- Your browser and version.
- Your operating system.
- The approximate time when the issue happened.
- The affected URL, scenario, or monitor name.

## Sending the report

Email your report to `clix@testclix.com`. If the issue is urgent, mention that in the subject line so we can prioritize it appropriately.

## Suggested subject line

```text
Bug Report: <short issue summary>
```
