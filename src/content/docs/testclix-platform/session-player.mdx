The **Session Player** is a core functionality that enables the replay of user sessions recorded via the recorder.
It is exclusively available for **[Website Scenario](../website-scenario/overview)** tests.

Unlike traditional video players, the Session Player is not time-based but rather **event-based**.
It navigates through the chronological history of events registered during the recording process.
