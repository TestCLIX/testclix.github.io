## Record Tests Without Code

The **Codeless Recorder** lets you create **[Website Scenario](/website-scenario/overview)** tests by interacting with your website in a real browser streamed from the cloud.
You do not need to install anything on your computer, prepare a local environment, or add a browser plugin to start recording.

Instead of writing automation scripts, maintaining selectors by hand, or building a QA framework from scratch, you simply perform the actions you want to test - click, type, navigate, submit - and TestCLIX records them as repeatable test steps.
Because the recorder runs remotely, teams can start using it immediately without desktop tools, browser extensions, or automation plugins.

## Why It Matters

The recorder is designed to reduce the cost of testing while making monitoring accessible to non-developers.

- **Lower QA cost:** business teams can define and maintain important monitoring flows without relying on engineering time for every test.
- **Faster feedback:** critical journeys can be recorded once and then replayed automatically after every release or on a schedule.
- **Shared visibility:** business owners and product teams can independently verify whether promised flows actually work in production.
- **Automates manual checks:** teams can turn repetitive browser checks that were previously hard to automate and often done by hand into repeatable monitoring flows.
- **Less code maintenance:** you avoid the ongoing overhead of building and maintaining traditional browser automation scripts for many common checks.

## Who Benefits Most

The recorder is especially useful when the people who know the business flow best are not the people writing code.

- **Business owners:** validate checkout, signup, lead generation, or account flows without learning test automation.
- **Product managers:** confirm that releases support the expected user journey before or after deployment.
- **Marketing teams:** monitor landing pages, forms, and conversion paths after content or campaign changes.
- **QA teams:** cover more regression scenarios faster and spend less time on repetitive manual verification.

## How Recording Works

When you start a recording session, TestCLIX launches a browser in the cloud and streams it to your screen.

As you interact with the page, the recorder captures actions such as:

- clicks,
- typing,
- navigation,
- and changes in page state.

Those actions become a reusable scenario that you can run manually or on a schedule from different locations.

## What You Get After Recording

Once a scenario is recorded, it becomes much more than a one-time QA exercise.

- You can schedule it for continuous monitoring.
- You can replay failures in the **[Session Player](/testclix-platform/session-player)**.
- You can compare visual output with **[Pixel Comparison](/website-scenario/pixel-comparison)**.
- You can add resilience features such as **[Page Structure Flexibility](/website-scenario/page-structure-flexibility)**.

This means the recorder is not just a test creation tool - it is the starting point for ongoing production monitoring.

## Best Use Cases

Use the Codeless Recorder when you want to protect flows such as:

- user login,
- checkout and payment steps,
- account registration,
- lead forms,
- and admin or dashboard workflows.

These are the paths where a silent breakage can directly affect revenue, support load, or customer trust.
